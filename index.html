About Baseball Bros

Baseball Bros is a simple, fast-paced baseball game that's easy to learn and has lots of crazy hijinks! With online multiplayer gameplay, franchise mode, fun pitching, and so much more!

Controls: Use either the arrow keys or WASD to control your bro. Space bar will throw the ball, dive, slide, or change directions when you're running the bases.

Be sure to follow us on social media for the latest updates and info!

FAQ

Why isn't the game loading?

Check your internet connection and make sure our site isn't blocked. Also, disable all ad blockers.

How do I play?

Use your arrow keys and space bar! The rest is easy—pick your play and go!

Which browser should I use?

Chrome is recommended. Baseball Bros has also been tested on Firefox and Safari, but is not guaranteed to work on them.

Are there more Bros games?

Yes! Check out Football Bros, Basket Bros, Soccer Bros, and Wrestle Bros. Also check out Web games at FreeGames.io.
